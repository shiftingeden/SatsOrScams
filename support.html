Frequently asked questions

I bought the tip / VIP but it's not showing.
Open Help → Restore Purchases in the app. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID used for the purchase.
How do I reset my progress?
Help → Reset Progress. This clears scores, coins, achievements, and unlocked items on this device. It cannot be undone.
There's no sound.
The game respects the silent switch and mixes with your own music. Flip the ring/silent switch off and check the volume. Music can also be changed in the Store's MUSIC tab.
Is my data collected?
No. Everything stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy.
Is any of this real financial advice?
No. Sats or Scams is a satire of crypto culture. There's no real cryptocurrency, no wallet, and no real trading. Please don't get liquidated in real life.
