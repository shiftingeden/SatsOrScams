Privacy Policy

Sats or Scams · Last updated July 11, 2026

Short version: Sats or Scams does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. Sats or Scams is a single-player arcade game that runs entirely on your device. We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal information, and the app does not create a user account.

Data stored on your device

Your progress — high scores, coins ("sats"), achievements, unlocked items, and settings — is saved locally on your device only. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. Deleting the app removes this data.

Analytics and tracking

We do not use any analytics tools, advertising networks, or third-party tracking SDKs. The app does not track you across other apps or websites.

In-app purchases

Sats or Scams offers one optional in-app purchase (a small "tip"). Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment card, billing details, or Apple ID. Apple's handling of that transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. We do not knowingly gather data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
